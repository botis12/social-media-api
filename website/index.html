Why this works

Most programmes fail because nobody measures anything.

You've probably run a plan off the internet before. It felt hard, you got sore, and three months later you still had no idea whether you were actually faster, stronger, or just more tired.

I coach the other way round. We start with a testing session that gives us real numbers — sprint splits, jump height, top-end strength, movement quality. Those numbers decide what your training block looks like. Then we retest and the numbers decide the next one.

It means you always know whether the work is working. It also means I can't hide behind vague talk about "feeling athletic" — the retest either moves or it doesn't.

  • Programming built from your assessment, never copy-pasted
  • Video review on every main lift, every week
  • Testing every 8–12 weeks so progress is provable
  • Direct access to me — you are not handed to a junior coach

How it works

Four steps. No mystery, no lock-in.

From first message to first retest is usually about ten weeks. Here's exactly what happens in between.

01

Enquire

Send me your sport, your timeline and what's stalled. I reply personally — usually within a working day — with an honest read on whether I'm the right coach for it.

02

Assess & test

A full movement screen plus baseline testing: sprint splits, jump height, strength markers, injury history. This is the only session that isn't training.

03

Train & check in

Your block lands in the app with video for every movement. You train, you log it, I review your lifts and adjust. Weekly check-in, direct line to me between them.

04

Retest

Every 8–12 weeks we run the same tests again and put the numbers side by side. That comparison — not a feeling — decides what the next block looks like.
