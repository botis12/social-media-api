How I coach

Three rules I don't bend.

Everything else — exercise selection, set counts, which app we use — is negotiable. These aren't.

01

Test, don't assume

Every athlete gets baseline numbers before a single set is programmed, and retests on a fixed schedule. If a block didn't move the number, I own that and we change it.

02

Technique before load

I review video on your main lifts every week. Not to be pedantic — because the athlete who can hold position under fatigue is the one still training in March.

03

Fit the plan to the life

Three good sessions you'll actually complete beat five perfect ones you'll abandon. Shift work, travel, exams, a new baby — tell me, and the block gets built around it.
